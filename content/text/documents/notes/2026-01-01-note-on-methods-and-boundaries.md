Audience: beginners exploring [divination](../_index.md) practices.

Learning goal: describe common methods and set clear boundaries for use.

Divination methods include casting lots, interpreting cards, or reading natural
patterns. Many practitioners set boundaries, such as avoiding medical or legal
advice and focusing on reflective guidance.

Check for understanding: Why might someone set boundaries around [divination](../_index.md) use?
