## **Preparation (Traditional)**

- Construct the ritual circle, inscribed with divine names (typically in Latin or Hebrew transliteration).
    
- Prepare the **Triangle of Art** for the spirit to appear within.
    
- Have the **Seal of Buer** ready, either worn on your person (as a ring or medallion) or displayed within the triangle.
    
- Prepare incense (typically frankincense or a Mars-aligned mixture).
    
- Tools include: sword or wand, consecrated parchment, ritual garments, and a book or document to receive the spirit’s answers.
    
- The operation is to be done **during the day and hour of Mars**, ideally when the **Sun is in Sagittarius**.
    

---

## **Opening Exorcism and Purification**

Begin with:

> _“O Lord God, who art seated upon the Cherubim and Seraphim, look down upon thy servant who places his trust in thee. Cleanse my heart and lips, O Lord, as Thou didst cleanse the lips of Thy prophet Isaiah with a burning coal, in the sight of the angel. Strengthen me, O Lord, and purify me, that I may worthily pronounce Thy holy words.”_

Sprinkle holy water and say:

> _“I exorcise thee, O creature of water, that thou mayest cast away all the power of the enemy, and that thou mayest become healthful unto man, against all the snares and illusions of the wicked spirits.”_

Incense the circle and triangle.

---

## **Primary Conjuration (Buer)**

> _“I conjure thee, O thou spirit Buer, by the power of the supreme and immortal God, who created heaven, earth, and the abyss. I conjure thee by the name of the living God—El, Elohim, Adonai, Tzabaoth, Elion, Jah, Ehyeh-Asher-Ehyeh, Shaddai—that thou appear before me now, visibly and in comely human form, without hurt or terror unto me or any creature, and obey my commands in all things that I shall require of thee.”_

> _“I exorcise thee, Buer, and adjure thee by all names of God, known and unknown, by AGLA, by On, by the Tetragrammaton, and by the ineffable name IHVH, that thou obey without delay and manifest yourself within this triangle, peacefully and visibly.”_

---

## **Invocation of Angelic Authority**

> _“I call thee in the name and by the power of Samael, angel of Mars, who is set over thee. By his governance and the will of the Most High, I command thee: appear swiftly and without deceit.”_

---

## **Displaying the Seal**

Hold up or reveal the Seal of Buer, saying:

> _“Behold the seal of thy office and obedience. By it and by the sacred bond between thee and the powers of heaven, I command thy appearance. Let the bond be honored, and let thy answers be true.”_

---

## **Welcome and Charge**

If the spirit appears:

> _“Welcome, O noble President Buer. I have called you forth in the name of the Most High. I ask that you speak truthfully and fulfill the charge given: to reveal wisdom, to teach, and to provide healing and understanding of what is hidden. Obey and you shall return in peace.”_

---

## **Interrogation / Request**

At this point, speak plainly and command:

- Ask for revelation of illness, or identification of concealed harm.
    
- Request insight into moral or natural philosophy.
    
- Ask for a teaching related to your purpose.
    
- Request the influence of good familiars (loyal spirits or symbolic assistants).
    

Example:

> _“I request, O Buer, that you reveal to me the root of the ailment that afflicts the system of [X], and instruct me in the means of restoration and correct philosophical alignment.”_

---

## **License to Depart**

Once the work is complete:

> _“Buer, noble President, I thank thee for thy presence and answers. Go now in peace to thy place, and let there be peace between thee and me, and between thy legions and my house. Depart in the name of the Most High, and harm no creature on thy way.”_

> _“I license thee to depart, and may the peace of the Most High be ever between us. Farewell.”_

Wait in silence. When the presence has receded, you may break the circle and conclude the rite.

The following [[describing|describes]] the [[ritual]] for [[conjuring]] [[Glasya-Labolas]], as [[recording|recorded]] in the [[Ars Goetia]] and other [[Gothic text|Gothic texts]].


## **Preparation:**

- Perform full Solomonic protective rituals:
	
	- Ritual bathing
		
	- Clean garments
		
	- Consecrated tools: sword, wand, pentacle
		
	- Circle with divine names inscribed (Tetragrammaton, Elohim, Adonai, Elion, etc.)
		
	- Triangle of Art placed at proper distance
		
	- Seal of Glasya-Labolas on parchment or metal
		
	- Protective incense (e.g., mastic or frankincense)
		
	- **Strong adherence to timing**: Mars hour, ideally in waning Moon (to reduce power overflow)

---

## **Opening Purification:**

Sprinkle holy water in circle:

> *“Purge me with hyssop, and I shall be clean; wash me, and I shall be whiter than snow. Let me stand in Thy presence undefiled, O Lord of Hosts, that I may walk safely amidst the spirits.”*

Incense the triangle:

> *“Let this smoke be a barrier and a beacon; let no deception pass it, and let the summoned find form within it.”*

---

## **Primary Conjuration:**

> *“I conjure thee, O thou mighty spirit Glasya-Labolas, by the ineffable name of the Most High, EL, ELOHIM, ADONAI, EHEIEH, TETRAGRAMMATON, by whom all things are set in order and by whose command thou art bound to thy office. Come forth now and appear before me, in fair and comely form, without harm or terror, and do that which I shall request.”*

---

## **Secondary Invocation (Angel Authority):**

> *“I call thee under the governance of Samael, Angel of Mars, who is set above thy dominion. By the chain of obedience and the seal of thy station, I compel thee to appear, to speak truly, and to obey without deceit.”*

---

## **Display of the Seal:**

Reveal the seal of Glasya-Labolas. Speak:

> *“By this seal I mark our boundary. By this mark, thou knowest thy place. Let no lie pass from thy lips, and let no signal escape this circuit. Show thyself, O President of Blood and Strategist of Conflict.”*

---

## **Charge and Interrogation:**

Use extreme caution in phrasing.

Example request:

> *“Reveal to me, Glasya-Labolas, that which lies hidden in the hearts of those who would betray me. Show me the trace of violence in the system I must pass through. Teach me the architecture of deception, that I may survive it.”*

**Avoid general invocations of harm.** If violence is named, be exact. Glasya-Labolas does not moderate intensity unless ordered to.

---

## **License to Depart:**

> *“Glasya-Labolas, I thank thee for thy presence and thy answers. Let there be peace between us, and let thy form now return to its place. Do no harm to creature, flesh, or field on thy way. By the power of the Most High, I release thee. Go in order.”*

---

## **Closing:**

- Burn or secure the seal.
	
- Re-cast protective banishing (e.g., LBRP or Psalm 91).
	
- Re-center yourself before interacting with others.
	
- Record any signals or residues carefully.
